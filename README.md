## Overview

A consulting-style strategy for a large enterprise. It builds a portfolio of AI use cases, scores them on impact, feasibility and risk, checks data readiness, estimates the business case for the top priorities, and lays out a 24-month roadmap with governance, delivered as a 15-slide deck.

## Business problem

A large Moroccan bank wants to adopt AI but doesn't know where to start, which use cases create value, whether its data is ready, or how to scale pilots safely.

## Key points

- Use-case portfolio (churn, fraud, credit risk, document AI, customer agent…)
- Scoring matrix: business impact × feasibility × risk → priority ranking
- Data-readiness and AI-maturity assessment (level 1 experimentation → level 5 transformation)
- Business case for the top 3: investment, savings, ROI, payback
- Roadmap: 0–3 months quick wins → 3–6 production → 6–12 scale → 12–24 transformation
- Responsible-AI and governance section; 15-slide executive deck
